Privacy and data use

How project information is handled

Information read

The authorized Sheets and Calendar records may include customer identifiers, names, contact details, service addresses, package and sales amounts, recorded payments, appointment dates, service numbering, pest descriptions and appointment notes. OAuth credentials are stored separately from reporting data.

Purposes

The project uses this information for management reporting, customer lookup, payment follow-up lists, appointment planning, location analysis, source-quality checks and evaluation of its predictive models. Derived records are used to train and evaluate business prediction models.

Processing services

Google Cloud hosts the restricted data, analytical tables, application, model evidence and credentials. The generative AI assistant was removed on 1 November 2026; the current workspace does not send question context to a language-model service. Sanitized service-address text is sent to Geoapify for geocoding; Geoapify is an external address-lookup provider.

Internal workspace progress

Staff can record review statuses, team notes and weekly capacity assumptions in a separate private Google Cloud store. These records coordinate work; they do not change Google source spreadsheets, settle payments, prove message delivery or send messages to customers.

Who can access information

The management workspace is restricted through Google sign-in and its authorized-user permissions. Publishing this information site does not publish customer records, source spreadsheets, individual experimental scores or credentials.

Retention

Project snapshots, geocoding caches, release audit records and model research outputs are retained for project operation and validation. There is currently no single automatic deletion deadline across these stores. The project administrator controls review and removal of retained copies.

Revocation and requests

The source-account owner can revoke the Google connection in Google account permissions. This stops new authorized access but does not automatically delete retained project copies or the original Google records. Contact the project administrator about review, correction or removal of project-held information.
