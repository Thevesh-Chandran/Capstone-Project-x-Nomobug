Capstone x Nomobug

Management reporting and research

What the project does

Capstone x Nomobug reads authorized Google Sheets and Google Calendar records to prepare sales and booking reports, customer lookup, payment follow-up lists, location analysis and experimental forecasts for Nomobug management and capstone evaluation.

Source access

The source connection requests read-only Sheets and Calendar access. It does not edit source spreadsheets, change appointments or automatically contact customers.

Private workspace

Customer information and business figures are held in the restricted management workspace. These information pages do not grant access to that workspace.

Experimental predictions

Historical model results are not guarantees of future performance. Staff should verify source records and use human judgment before acting.
