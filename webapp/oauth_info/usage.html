Use and limitations

Information for authorized staff

Intended use

The system supports authorized management work and capstone evaluation. Public information pages do not grant access to the management workspace.

Source records

Displayed figures depend on source completeness and freshness. Recorded payment balances are not an independently verified cash ledger. A scheduled appointment does not prove that treatment was completed.

Model outputs

Experimental predictions, geographic associations and historical performance require human review. They do not establish causation or determine customer warranty eligibility by themselves.

Business terms

This information page does not change Nomobug customer contracts, package terms or warranty rules. The application does not automatically contact customers.
