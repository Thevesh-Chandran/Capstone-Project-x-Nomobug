Project purpose

Connect Sales, Calendar, contract reminder records and environmental context to management reporting, customer follow-up and experimentally evaluated predictions.

Reading order

1. Data Sources — what each source records and what one row means. 2. Data Cleaning — how types, duplicate records, dates and service links are treated. 3. Outcome Labels and Partitions — what is predicted and which records are used for fitting, validation and assessment. 4. Predictors — every input, its meaning, observed association and removal-test evidence. 5. Pest Return Model — configuration, training code and historical classification results. 6. Weekly Planning Models — separate booking and appointment-hour forecasts. 7. Model Experiments — historical alternatives, inputs and results.

These notebooks replay pinned aggregate evidence. They do not train a new model, change its weights or manufacture future results. Production source commands and code paths are identified in the relevant chapter. The source snapshots remain private in the project.
