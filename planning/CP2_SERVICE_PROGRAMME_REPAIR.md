form one residential three-session programme. The two sales retain their own
identifiers, amounts and payment records. Programme totals are not additional
sales or cash received.

paid service stages. The final two stages are funded by the Upsell 2x sale.

These dates are recorded appointments, not proof of completed treatment.

sequence labels receive this correction. Source changes fail validation rather
than silently attaching another customer's event. Ordinary programmes remain
separate. Tests check the programme exists exactly once and all three stages
are matched once to their expected funding sale.

through thirty days after the recorded final service, regardless of pest.
Commercial clients and residential 1x remain ineligible.

the corrected programme service facts. Financial reporting retains sales grain.

for reproducibility. This improves operational reporting and future modelling
inputs; it does not retroactively repair the frozen model's package features
or create a new accuracy result.
